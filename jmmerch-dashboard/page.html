Tour inventory overview The dial shows how many John has on hand now (the On Tour stock in Shopify, as of the last sync). The right end of the dial is everything he's had this run: what he started with plus any top-ups (worked out as on hand now + sold at shows since the run began). A run starts after a break of more than 14 days between shows.

The coloured band around it shows how long that lasts at the booked shows:
• red = less than 3½ weeks of sales
• yellow = less than 3 months
• green = more than 3 months

The card colour is the verdict. A shirt turns red or yellow if any one size runs out, even when the total looks fine.

How projections work
Projected sales = units per 100 tickets × expected tickets at each show.

Units per 100 tickets comes from each product's last 10 shows with sales. Expected tickets = tickets sold so far, or 70% of capacity if that's higher.

🎟️Spend per fan Merch gross ÷ tickets sold, in Canadian dollars.

Actual attendance isn't recorded, so "per head" is per ticket sold.

Only played shows with both a ticket count and merch sales are included. Gross comes from point-of-sale sales, or from the calendar when a show has none linked.

💰Profit Profit = sales − cost of goods − venue merch cuts, in Canadian dollars, before tax.

Cost of goods uses each product's unit cost in Notion, or the cost recorded in the sales spreadsheet.

Venue cuts are only included where they were recorded (the spreadsheet years). Shopify sales since October 2026 don't record them yet, so profit at venues that take a cut is overstated.

Products with no unit cost are marked and left out of profit.

How these numbers are worked out

All data comes from the Notion Merch Inventory System: Products, Shows, Sales and Data Issues. Shopify stock and orders reach Notion through the weekly sync; show dates, capacities and tickets come read-only from John's calendar.

Units per 100 tickets uses each product's last 10 played shows that had point-of-sale sales and a ticket count, starting from the first show where that product sold. Refunds are subtracted.

Road run-out walks forward through every confirmed show in the next 180 days, adding each show's forecast, and names the first show where the total passes road stock. Web run-out assumes the last 8 weeks' pace continues.

Sales in other currencies are converted to Canadian dollars by Shopify at the time of sale. Bundles and discontinued products are left out of the forecasts.
