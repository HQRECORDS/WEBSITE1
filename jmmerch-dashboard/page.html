How to read this page

On hand is John's tour stock in Shopify at the last sync.

Needed is what we expect him to sell at the confirmed shows in the period you picked: units per 100 tickets (from each product's last 10 shows with sales) × expected tickets (tickets sold so far, or 70% of capacity if that's higher). Offers and holds aren't counted.

Short is how many more he'd need. For shirts it's added up size by size, because a spare L can't cover a missing XL. That's why a shirt can be short even when on hand is more than needed.

Tight means he has enough, but less than 20% to spare.

💰Money Profit = sales − HST/GST − cost of goods − venue merch cuts, in Canadian dollars.

HST / GST: show prices include sales tax, so it's backed out of show sales rung up with no tax recorded, at the show's province rate (Ontario 13%, Nova Scotia 14%, NB/NL/PEI 15%, other provinces 5% GST). PST isn't backed out, and shows outside Canada have none.

Cost of goods uses each product's unit cost in Notion, or the cost recorded in the sales spreadsheet.

Venue cuts come from the sales spreadsheet where recorded. For Shopify sales they're worked out from each show's Merch Terms in Notion (for example 85/15 = 15% to the venue), on sales before tax. Blank terms count as no cut.

Card fees use Shopify Payments' standard rates on Shopify sales: 2.6% in person, 2.9% online. Cash sales have no fee, so this runs a little high.

Products with no unit cost are marked and left out of profit.

How these numbers are worked out

All data comes from the Notion Merch Inventory System: Products, Shows, Sales and Data Issues. Shopify stock and orders reach Notion through the weekly sync; show dates, capacities and tickets come read-only from John's calendar.

Units per 100 tickets uses each product's last 10 played shows that had point-of-sale sales and a ticket count, starting from the first show where that product sold. Refunds are subtracted.

Road run-out walks forward through every confirmed show in the next 180 days, adding each show's forecast, and names the first show where the total passes road stock. Web run-out assumes the last 8 weeks' pace continues.

Sales in other currencies are converted to Canadian dollars by Shopify at the time of sale. Bundles and discontinued products are left out of the forecasts.
